Undergraduate internship

KAIST undergraduate students interested in internships, independent studies, or URP (Undergraduate Research Program) opportunities are welcome to contact us. Please email your CV, cover letter, and portfolio to express your interest. Students from other universities, such as Jaeyeon Kim, can also apply through the same process.
